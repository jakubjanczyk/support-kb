The three problems

hint: the booking-v3 form tells people to type the month first. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at day-first tenants follow the hint, and the form reads what they typed the tenant’s way. The date is wrong before the parser sees it, so the parser fix leaves it wrong. Only reports sent through the form at booking-v3 tenants belong here.

One-day move: the date moves by one day, not a month. The desk already noted these (desk-notes.json, oneDayMove) and counts them under parser, where they are filed; the note under the table keeps them visible. They read like a time-zone or day-boundary shift: one meeting showing Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto, an invite arriving before its booking. A parser fix does not touch them.
